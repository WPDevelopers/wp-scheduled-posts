## What is SchedulePress?

SchedulePress is a WordPress content-scheduling tool: a drag-and-drop **editorial calendar**, flexible **scheduling** (with a missed-schedule fix in Pro), and **automatic social sharing** to Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Instagram, Medium, and Threads (Google Business Profile in Pro). It works in the block editor, the classic editor, and Elementor.

> Formerly known as **WP Scheduled Posts** — same plugin, rebranded. Your content is unaffected. Pro features live in the sibling `wp-scheduled-posts-pro` plugin.
